GETTING STARTED WITH LOCALIZER

Thank you for installing Localizer. We hope you will find it useful and handy. To make your start easier, we have prepared several simple and short instructions:
  1. First of all, you may want to see Localizer at work. To do so, open one of our sample applications and see how it is organized. The source code of the Localizer samples is in the [My Documents]\KorzhLocalizer\Samples\ folder (you can open it with the "Open Samples folder" item in Localizer's Start menu). Just open one of the sample projects in your Delphi IDE and compile it. Once it compiles and runs, try switching the language and see how it works.
  2. After that you can try to apply Localizer to your own project. Read the following knowledge base article to get an idea of how to start.
  3. To proceed further with your project localization you can read the "How to translate using Language Manager" and "How to use Localizer in your code" articles.
Please note! If anything described above goes wrong, don't hesitate to contact us right away. Our support team will be glad to help you with any question.
