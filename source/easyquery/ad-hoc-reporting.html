THE PROBLEM

"We need to change 3 reports and create 5 new."

Have you heard that from your customer? Then you know where the time goes: every new report is a ticket, a query, a page, a release — and next month, another round of changes.

With EasyQuery, your users build, change and save the reports themselves: they pick the columns, add the conditions, group and total the data, see it as a table or a chart, and export it. You build the reporting module once.

WHAT YOUR USERS GET

  • A list of saved reports, their own and shared ones
  • A visual editor for columns, conditions, sorting and grouping, in plain language
  • Totals, charts and export to Excel, PDF or CSV
  • Only the data you let them see

HOW IT FITS YOUR STACK

EasyQuery builds the data model from your Entity Framework DbContext, keeps the reports in a store you choose (your own database table in the sample), and checks every action with your ASP.NET Core authentication: who may create, change or delete a report is up to you.

The UI is EasyQuery.JS in your Razor pages, MVC views, or a React, Angular, Vue or Blazor frontend.

builder.Services.AddEasyQuery()
    .UseSqlManager()
    .AddDefaultExporters();
...
app.MapEasyQuery(options => {
    options.Endpoint = "/api/adhoc-reporting";
    options.SaveNewQuery = true;
    options.UseDbContextWithoutIdentity<AppDbContext>();
    options.UseQueryStore(manager => new ReportStore(manager.Services));
    options.UseDefaultAuthProvider();
});
