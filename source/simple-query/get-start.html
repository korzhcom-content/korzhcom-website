GETTING STARTED WITH
SIMPLEQUERY

Thank you for installing SimpleQuery. We hope you will find it useful and handy. To make your start easier, we have prepared several simple and short instructions:
  1. First of all, you may want to see SimpleQuery at work. To do so, open one of our sample applications and see how it is organized. The source code of the SimpleQuery samples is in the [InstallDir]\sq\demos\ folder. Just open one of the sample projects in your Delphi IDE and compile it. The best demo project to start with is KQB. Once it compiles and runs, try changing or adding some conditions and/or columns, load another query and see how it works.
  2. After that, you can try SimpleQuery in your own project. Just read the "Getting started" topic in the SimpleQuery help file and follow the instructions there.
Please note! If anything described above goes wrong, don't hesitate to contact us right away. Our support team will be glad to help you with any question.
