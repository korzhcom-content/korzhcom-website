HOW IT WORKS

1

Your data model

EasyQuery builds the data model from your Entity Framework DbContext or straight from your database schema. You decide which tables, fields and relations your users see, and what they are called.

2

Your users' queries

Users pick columns and add conditions in the EasyQuery widgets, in plain language: no SQL, no knowledge of your tables. The widgets live in your pages, in your look.

3

Your rules

EasyQuery turns the query into SQL or LINQ and runs it in your application, under your own authentication and access rules. The results go back to a grid, a chart or an export.
